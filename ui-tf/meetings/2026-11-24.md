### [UI] Add `shui:hidden` to control rendering independently of validation

PR: [#1363](https://github.com/w3c/data-shapes/pull/1363)

The task force agreed that a property shape declaring `shui:hidden false` will still be rendered when its property group declares `shui:hidden true`.

The behaviour of the read-only predicate should also be aligned with this approach.

### [UI] Add messages and their anchoring

PR: [#1395](https://github.com/w3c/data-shapes/pull/1395)

Edmond: I don't agree with using the global configuration as a mechanism for declaring messages and alerts on SHACL Renderer forms. I think that, as with SHACL Core, composing shapes containing UI annotations with validation shapes is the appropriate approach. This is standard SHACL shape management and does not require introducing a new ad hoc mechanism in SHACL UI.

Since the discussion was just between Ieben and Edmond, we decided to continue this topic offline.
