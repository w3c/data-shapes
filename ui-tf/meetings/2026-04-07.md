### Autocomplete / Enum Discussion

The group discussed using constraints to retrieve the set of possible IRIs for autocomplete and enum-style editors.

A key question was how labels for those IRIs should then be resolved. The proposal and examples in Issue #846 were referenced during the discussion.

Bergi suggested separating two concerns that are currently coupled in the proposal: label resolution on the one hand, and searching or filtering on the other. He also noted that node expressions could serve as an alternative to using a SPARQL `SERVICE` clause.

Bergi further raised the question of whether protocols other than SPARQL, such as REST, should also be supported. The view was that REST could potentially be supported through a custom node expression, and that this could be documented as a non-normative pattern.

Edmond asked how a custom REST-based node expression returning JSON would be mapped into RDF.

Ieben said that he had initially understood label resolution as a separate algorithm for resolving labels, although that did not mean he opposed the current proposal tied to autocomplete and enum handling.

Daniel clarified that the current proposal is not intended to replace the previously discussed label resolution work. Rather, it provides a way to override the label resolution algorithm in cases where more specificity and control are needed through SPARQL.

### Action

Daniel to update the proposal to separate label resolution from searching and filtering.
