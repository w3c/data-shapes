#### Structuring the Document

- The work should be split into two separate PRs:
  1. **Definition of widget scoring**, self-contained.
  2. **Definitions of widget scores and shapes**, separate from the scoring definition.
- When defining widget scoring:
  - Do not reference specific widgets defined elsewhere in the document.
  - Keep the scoring definition general and implementation-agnostic.
- We should ensure the scoring system does **not** constrain implementors to a single processing strategy:
  - A common approach is to evaluate `shui:Score` instances from highest to lowest score, stopping at the first match.
  - However, implementors should also be able to continue evaluating all shui:Score instances if they want to offer multiple matching widgets for user or application choice.
- Gist notes referenced during meetings should be incorporated directly into the minutes.
- Next step: Create a gist draft for sub-group review.
