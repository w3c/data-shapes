## Autocomplete Widget

Gist: https://gist.github.com/danielbeeke/b8193dcabe79e9f864406ab6814698c4

Daniel noted that `shui:resultVariable` could potentially be requested through SHACL-SPARQL rather than being introduced as a term in the `shui` namespace.

The need for `shui:resultVariable` arises because `sh:select` node expressions are scalar-valued rather than row- or object-valued, and therefore can only return a single value.

Alternative proposal without `shui:resultVariable`:

https://gist.github.com/danielbeeke/b8193dcabe79e9f864406ab6814698c4?permalink_comment_id=6201775#gistcomment-6201775

Action: Daniel to develop a prototype for discussion at next week's meeting.

Bergi expressed concerns that the new proposal may not address all relevant use cases and that it introduces too much implicit behaviour ("magic").

Daniel clarified that the primary use case being targeted is autocomplete.

Edmond suggested that, rather than using a `SERVICE` clause within a select node expression, it would be preferable to define an explicit `shui:sparqlEndpoint`.

Action: Daniel and Bergi to prepare summaries of their respective viewpoints for discussion with the wider task force.
